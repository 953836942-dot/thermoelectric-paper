# TE Literature Radar Scoring Policy

Every candidate must first pass a thermoelectric relevance gate. Journal prestige cannot override a failed gate.

Weights: TE relevance 30, research quality 30, novelty 20, research fit 10, recency 10.

Quality tier bases: premier 28, elite 26, high 24 (approximately Advanced Functional Materials level), solid 16, standard 12, preprint 8. Peer-reviewed records may receive +1 for an abstract and +1 for DOI/stable identity plus URL, capped at 30. Preprints are capped at 10 quality points.

Peer-reviewed/unknown-status grades:
- A: total >=80, relevance >=24, and quality >=24 or novelty >=18.
- B: total >=65, relevance >=21, and quality >=18 or novelty >=15.
- C: total >=50 and relevance >=18.

Preprint grades are stricter:
- A: total >=78, relevance >=27, novelty >=18.
- B: total >=72, relevance >=25, novelty >=16.
- C: total >=60, relevance >=22, novelty >=14.

All preprints must display `Preprint — not peer reviewed`.
