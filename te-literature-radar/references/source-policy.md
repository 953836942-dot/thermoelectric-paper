# TE Literature Radar Source Policy

- Crossref supplies DOI and target-journal metadata. Target-journal ISSNs must be verified before configuration; never guess an ISSN.
- OpenAlex provides broad materials-science discovery and overlap coverage beyond the target-journal list.
- arXiv provides early/preprint discovery, especially for machine learning, AI, high-throughput screening, and emerging methods. Its papers are always labeled preprints and use stricter grade thresholds.
- RSS/Atom feeds are optional early-notification sources for trusted publishers, journals, or societies.
- V1 does not download paywalled PDFs.
- Missing abstracts remain metadata-only records. Codex must not infer experimental findings from title-only records.
- Source failures are recorded and other successful sources continue.
